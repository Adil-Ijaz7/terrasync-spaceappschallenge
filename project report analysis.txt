1. ⚠️ Critical Bugs & Gaps (Scan Findings jo foran theek karne hain)
🔴 Bug 1: Map Coordinates Click Mismatch (Sab se barra bug!)
Kahan hai: app/page.tsx vs app/api/analyze/route.ts
Masla: Frontend se body: JSON.stringify({ latitude: lat, longitude: lng }) bheja ja raha hai, jabke backend route mein likha hai:
typescript
const lat = typeof body.lat === 'number' ? body.lat : 26.7341;
const lng = typeof body.lng === 'number' ? body.lng : 67.7795;
Khatra: body.lat undefined hota hai, isliye map par kahin bhi click karo ya koi doosra sector chuno, system hamesha default Dadu coordinates par reset ho jata hai! Isko body.latitude ?? body.lat karna zaroori hai taake judges jab map par kisi bhi jagah click karein toh live change nazar aye.
🟡 Bug 2: Map Zoom Level & Camera Transition
components/MapComponent.tsx mein initial zoom zoom={6} hai (jo poora Sindh/Pakistan dikhata hai). Jab user kisi farm ya sector par click karta hai, camera zoom in nahi hota (zoom level 6 par hi rehta hai).
Fix: Click par smooth map.flyTo([lat, lng], 13) hona chahiye taake detective ki tarah satellite camera seedha khet ke andar zoom-in ho!
